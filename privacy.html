ChatShelf privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version

  • ChatShelf works locally in your browser.
  • It reads only the titles and links of chats that ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or DeepSeek already show you. It never reads or stores your messages.
  • Nothing leaves your device in the current version. Cloud sync (Pro) is planned; it is off until you sign in and turn it on.
  • We don't sell data, show ads or use analytics.

What ChatShelf stores on your device

Stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

  • Folders you create (name, colour, position).
  • For chats you add to folders, and recent chats shown in a platform's sidebar: the platform, the chat's link and ID, and its title.
  • Prompts you save (title, text, tags, colour).
  • Settings (for example which platforms are on, and the panel height).

Removing the extension deletes this data. You can export it any time as a JSON file from Settings.

Cloud sync (Pro, coming soon, optional)

If you sign in and turn on sync, the items listed above are copied to our database so your other devices can get them. That means folders, chat titles and links, prompts and settings. Message content is never part of it, because ChatShelf never has it.

  • Processor: Supabase (database and email sign-in). Data is protected so only your account can read or change it.
  • We keep your email address to sign you in and to tie your purchase to your account.
  • Payments are handled by our payment provider (merchant of record). We never see your card details.
  • Deletion: email us to delete your account and all synced data. Deleting the account removes every synced record.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of information received through ChatShelf adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use it only to provide ChatShelf's features, never transfer it for advertising, credit checks or any other purpose, and never let humans read it except with your permission, for security reasons, or where the law requires.

Children

ChatShelf is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Material changes are announced on this page before they take effect.
