Using ChatShelf

  • ChatShelf is a browser extension that organises your AI chats into folders and stores prompts. The free version works without an account.
  • You may use it for personal and work purposes. Don't use it to break the terms of the AI services you use it with, or any law.
  • ChatShelf is an independent product. It is not made, endorsed or supported by OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or DeepSeek.

Your data

Your folders, chat links and prompts belong to you. The privacy policy explains what ChatShelf stores and where. You can export everything as a JSON file at any time.

Pro (coming soon)

Pro will add unlimited folders and chats, plus cloud sync. Prices, billing terms and refunds will be shown before you buy, and payments will be handled by our payment provider.

Changes to the AI sites

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek change their pages often. When a change stops the in-page panel from appearing, the side panel keeps working and we ship a fix as soon as we can. We can't promise any site will stay supported.

No warranty

ChatShelf is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for lost data or indirect damages from using it. Keep a backup export of anything important.
